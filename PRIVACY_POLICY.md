# Privacy Policy for Girvi Mobile

We collect and process the following data to operate the app:
- Account data (username, email) for authentication
- Business data you enter (customers, transactions, payments)
- Device push token (for notifications)

Data usage:
- To provide app functionality, reminders, and receipts you initiate
- We do not sell personal data

Storage:
- Data is stored on your device and on your server
- HTTPS is required in production

Third parties:
- WhatsApp deep links are opened when you tap send buttons
- Firebase Cloud Messaging is used for push notifications

Contact:
- For questions or deletion requests, contact the app administrator
